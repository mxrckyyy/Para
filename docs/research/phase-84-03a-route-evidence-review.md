## 3. Official source findings

### Cebu City LPTRP context

Based on prior research (Phase 27, Phase 63):

- Cebu City's Local Public Transport Route Plan (LPTRP) was approved by LTFRB (Board Resolution 186, Series of 2025).
- The LPTRP rationalizes routes into 40 approved routes (39 Class 2 + 1 Class 3 feeder).
- Traditional and developmental routes are subject to modernization compliance.
- Route codes like 03A, 04L, 13C are mentioned in LPTRP context but specific stop sequences are not documented in accessible sources.
- The LPTRP reorganizes the network into formal corridors; traditional route codes may be consolidated or replaced.

### LTFRB Region VII

- Show Cause Orders issued for 01K (Libreng Sakay program) confirm service existence.
- No route-level documentation with stop sequences found accessible.
- LTFRB website not accessible (HTTP 403).

### Key finding: LPTRP and traditional routes

The LPTRP rationalizes existing routes. Traditional jeepney codes (01K, 03A, 04L, 13C, 17B) may be:

- Retained as developmental routes under the new framework.
- Consolidated into new rationalized routes.
- Replaced by modern jeepney services with different codes.

The exact status of 03A under the LPTRP is not documented in any accessible source.
