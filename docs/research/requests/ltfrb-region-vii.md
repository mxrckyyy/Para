# Records Request Draft - LTFRB Region VII

## Requesting Institution

Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB) Region VII

## Records Sought

1. Current authorized PUJ route matrix for Cebu City.
2. Relevant franchise route descriptions for routes 01K, 03A, 04L, 13C, and 17B.
3. Current status of legacy routes 01K, 13C, 04L, 17B, and 03A under the LPTRP.
4. Relevant approved route amendments.
5. Available public records concerning replacement or rationalization of legacy routes.

## Purpose

Para is a Cebu jeepney route discovery application that needs verified route identities, endpoints, intermediate stops, and current operational status to present accurate information to commuters.

## Routes of interest

- 01K (Sambag II/SWU to SM and North Bus Terminal)
- 03A (Mabolo/Panagdait to Carbon Market)
- 04L (Lahug/JY Square to SM via Ayala)
- 13C (Talamban to Colon via Echavez and Sikatuna)
- 17B (Apas to Carbon via Escario and Osmeña Boulevard)

## Requested format

Electronic copies (PDF or spreadsheet) where available.
