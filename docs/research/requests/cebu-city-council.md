# Records Request Draft - Cebu City Council

## Requesting Institution

Cebu City Council (Sangguniang Panlungsod)

## Records Sought

1. Applicable LPTRP adoption ordinance.
2. Relevant amendments to Cebu City Ordinance No. 2000.
3. Supporting annexes for LPTRP adoption.
4. Public legislative records concerning public transportation route plans.
5. Any ordinance or resolution referencing specific jeepney route codes.

## Purpose

Para is a Cebu jeepney route discovery application that needs verified route identities, endpoints, intermediate stops, and current operational status to present accurate information to commuters.

## Routes of interest

- 01K, 03A, 04L, 13C, 17B
- Mabolo Central Loop routes

## Requested format

Electronic copies of ordinances and resolutions where available.

## Notes

This is a draft. It has not been submitted. Submission requires authorization from the project owner.
