## 5. Conflicting evidence

- Suroy.ph and Cebu Daily News Digital both associate `04L` with Lahug/JY Square and Ayala/SM.
- CommuteTour lists `04L` as “Lahug – Ayala” while Suroy.ph and Cebu Daily News Digital include SM. This may reflect different common endpoints or guide simplification; it is not resolved by this review.
- No reviewed source establishes a complete stop list or exact operating path.
- No reviewed source provides fare, schedule, or travel-time data.

## 6. Resolved uncertainties

No uncertainty was fully resolved. The route code and general corridor remain supported by multiple secondary sources, but the exact stop sequence, direction, fare, schedule, travel time, and geometry remain unsupported.

- Whether the route path follows the documented corridor or a different road path.
- Whether fare, schedule, or travel-time data can be obtained from an official source.
- Whether the `01K` endpoint should be `SWU (Sambag II)` or `Urgello in Sambag II`.
